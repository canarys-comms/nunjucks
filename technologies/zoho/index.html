Questions teams usually ask

Frequently asked questions about Zoho

Key questions when adopting Zoho in an enterprise environment.

Where does Zoho fit in an enterprise architecture?

Zoho should own a clear responsibility around Zoho CRM/One apps, custom workflows, Deluge, integrations and data consistency. A useful placement is one where it directly supports sales CRM or customer service and the integration boundaries are explicit.

What should be standardized when adopting Zoho?

Standardize the repeatable parts first: app and field ownership and workflow standards, plus reusable patterns from Zoho CRM implementation and Zoho One configuration. Leave workload-specific choices closer to the teams that own them.

What problems commonly appear when Zoho scales?

Common signals include apps are enabled independently without a shared process model, custom functions become difficult to maintain, and CRM data is duplicated in external spreadsheets. These usually indicate that Zoho adoption has outgrown the original team-level operating model.

How does Canarys help with Zoho?

Canarys supports Zoho CRM implementation, Zoho One configuration, workflow automation, and Deluge customization, then connects the result to enterprise governance and operations so Zoho can be maintained as a capability rather than a one-off implementation.
