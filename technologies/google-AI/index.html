Questions teams usually ask

Frequently asked questions about Google AI

Key questions when adopting Google AI in an enterprise environment.

Where does Google AI fit in an enterprise architecture?

Google AI should own a clear responsibility around Vertex AI, model selection, managed training, enterprise data, deployment and evaluation. A useful placement is one where it directly supports Vertex AI platforms or GenAI applications and the integration boundaries are explicit.

What should be standardized when adopting Google AI?

Standardize the repeatable parts first: project and IAM structure and data boundaries, plus reusable patterns from Vertex AI architecture and generative AI solution engineering. Leave workload-specific choices closer to the teams that own them.

What problems commonly appear when Google AI scales?

Common signals include foundation-model features are adopted without evaluation criteria, data remains outside the governed AI workflow, and projects use inconsistent model and endpoint patterns. These usually indicate that Google AI adoption has outgrown the original team-level operating model.

How does Canarys help with Google AI?

Canarys supports Vertex AI architecture, generative AI solution engineering, training pipelines, and model and endpoint management, then connects the result to enterprise governance and operations so Google AI can be maintained as a capability rather than a one-off implementation.
