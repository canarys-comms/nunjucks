Questions teams usually ask

Frequently asked questions about Artificial Intelligence

Key questions when adopting Artificial Intelligence in an enterprise environment.

Where does Artificial Intelligence fit in an enterprise architecture?

Artificial Intelligence should own a clear responsibility around problem framing, enterprise context, model choice, integration and operational control. A useful placement is one where it directly supports decision support or classification and routing and the integration boundaries are explicit.

What should be standardized when adopting Artificial Intelligence?

Standardize the repeatable parts first: data and access boundaries and quality criteria, plus reusable patterns from AI strategy and use-case design and data and context architecture. Leave workload-specific choices closer to the teams that own them.

What problems commonly appear when Artificial Intelligence scales?

Common signals include AI is selected before the decision problem is clear, different model types are treated as interchangeable, and output is disconnected from the workflow that acts on it. These usually indicate that Artificial Intelligence adoption has outgrown the original team-level operating model.

How does Canarys help with Artificial Intelligence?

Canarys supports AI strategy and use-case design, data and context architecture, model and solution engineering, and application integration, then connects the result to enterprise governance and operations so Artificial Intelligence can be maintained as a capability rather than a one-off implementation.
