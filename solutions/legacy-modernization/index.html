Questions teams usually ask

Frequently asked questions about Legacy Modernization

Key questions when planning Legacy Modernization.

What does Legacy Modernization mean in an enterprise context?

For Canarys, Legacy Modernization means using how to extract value from a legacy estate without losing behaviour the business still relies on to reduce dependence on ageing systems that still carry important business logic, data and operational knowledge. The emphasis is a working operating model rather than technology adoption by itself.

Where should a Legacy Modernization initiative start?

Start where business rules exist only in old code or unsupported technology narrows the support pool is already creating visible cost, delay or risk. A bounded starting point makes dependencies easier to understand and outcomes easier to measure.

What capabilities are usually involved in Legacy Modernization?

The mix varies, but common delivery areas include legacy discovery, business-rule extraction, strangler modernization, and interface modernization. The right combination depends on which layer is actually constraining the target state.

How can an enterprise avoid over-engineering Legacy Modernization?

Preserve stable capability, change the layer causing the constraint and use operating evidence before scaling. The aim is lower legacy dependency, preserved business behaviour, and safer phased transition, not a larger technology footprint.
