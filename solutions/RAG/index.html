Questions teams usually ask

Frequently asked questions about RAG

Key questions when planning RAG.

What does RAG mean in an enterprise context?

For Canarys, RAG means using content ingestion, chunking, embeddings, retrieval, authorization, generation and citation to ground generative AI in enterprise knowledge so answers can be traced to the information the organization actually owns. The emphasis is a working operating model rather than technology adoption by itself.

Where should a RAG initiative start?

Start where retrieval returns semantically similar but irrelevant content or documents are indexed without access rules is already creating visible cost, delay or risk. A bounded starting point makes dependencies easier to understand and outcomes easier to measure.

What capabilities are usually involved in RAG?

The mix varies, but common delivery areas include RAG architecture, content ingestion, retrieval engineering, and vector and hybrid search. The right combination depends on which layer is actually constraining the target state.

How can an enterprise avoid over-engineering RAG?

Preserve stable capability, change the layer causing the constraint and use operating evidence before scaling. The aim is more grounded answers, better enterprise knowledge access, and traceable source context, not a larger technology footprint.
