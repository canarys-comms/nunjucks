Questions teams usually ask

Frequently asked questions about ERP Modernization

Key questions when planning ERP Modernization.

What does ERP Modernization mean in an enterprise context?

For Canarys, ERP Modernization means using process standardization, clean core, extensions, data migration and surrounding-system integration to modernize the enterprise core without recreating years of custom process and integration debt on a new ERP platform. The emphasis is a working operating model rather than technology adoption by itself.

Where should a ERP Modernization initiative start?

Start where old customizations are carried forward by default or master data issues appear during migration is already creating visible cost, delay or risk. A bounded starting point makes dependencies easier to understand and outcomes easier to measure.

What capabilities are usually involved in ERP Modernization?

The mix varies, but common delivery areas include ERP assessment, process redesign, target ERP architecture, and data migration. The right combination depends on which layer is actually constraining the target state.

How can an enterprise avoid over-engineering ERP Modernization?

Preserve stable capability, change the layer causing the constraint and use operating evidence before scaling. The aim is cleaner enterprise core, lower customization debt, and better master-data control, not a larger technology footprint.
