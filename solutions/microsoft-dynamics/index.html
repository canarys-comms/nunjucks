Questions teams usually ask

Frequently asked questions about Microsoft Dynamics

Key questions when planning Microsoft Dynamics.

What does Microsoft Dynamics mean in an enterprise context?

For Canarys, Microsoft Dynamics means using CRM or ERP process, Dataverse, Power Platform, integrations and reporting to shape Microsoft Dynamics around customer and business processes while connecting the wider Microsoft and enterprise ecosystem. The emphasis is a working operating model rather than technology adoption by itself.

Where should a Microsoft Dynamics initiative start?

Start where CRM stages do not match the actual sales or service process or customizations duplicate what Power Platform could handle cleanly is already creating visible cost, delay or risk. A bounded starting point makes dependencies easier to understand and outcomes easier to measure.

What capabilities are usually involved in Microsoft Dynamics?

The mix varies, but common delivery areas include Dynamics implementation, process configuration, Power Platform extension, and Dataverse and integration. The right combination depends on which layer is actually constraining the target state.

How can an enterprise avoid over-engineering Microsoft Dynamics?

Preserve stable capability, change the layer causing the constraint and use operating evidence before scaling. The aim is better process adoption, connected customer data, and fewer manual workarounds, not a larger technology footprint.
