Questions teams usually ask

Frequently asked questions about Platform Engineering

Key questions when planning Platform Engineering.

What does Platform Engineering mean in an enterprise context?

For Canarys, Platform Engineering means using developer journeys, platform APIs, paved paths, golden templates and platform ownership to turn common infrastructure and delivery practices into self-service products that application teams can consume without rebuilding the mechanics. The emphasis is a working operating model rather than technology adoption by itself.

Where should a Platform Engineering initiative start?

Start where teams file tickets for routine environments or shared scripts have no product owner is already creating visible cost, delay or risk. A bounded starting point makes dependencies easier to understand and outcomes easier to measure.

What capabilities are usually involved in Platform Engineering?

The mix varies, but common delivery areas include platform product design, internal developer platform, golden paths, and infrastructure self-service. The right combination depends on which layer is actually constraining the target state.

How can an enterprise avoid over-engineering Platform Engineering?

Preserve stable capability, change the layer causing the constraint and use operating evidence before scaling. The aim is less developer waiting, more consistent engineering, and reusable platform capability, not a larger technology footprint.
