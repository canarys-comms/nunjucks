Questions teams usually ask

Frequently asked questions about AI & Intelligence

Key questions when planning AI & Intelligence.

What does AI & Intelligence mean in an enterprise context?

For Canarys, AI & Intelligence means using the decision, context, model, workflow and control points required to make intelligence operational to embed useful intelligence into enterprise decisions and workflows instead of treating AI as a separate destination. The emphasis is a working operating model rather than technology adoption by itself.

Where should a AI & Intelligence initiative start?

Start where AI pilots disconnected from daily work or data context too weak for reliable output is already creating visible cost, delay or risk. A bounded starting point makes dependencies easier to understand and outcomes easier to measure.

What capabilities are usually involved in AI & Intelligence?

The mix varies, but common delivery areas include AI opportunity design, data and context engineering, model and analytics engineering, and application integration. The right combination depends on which layer is actually constraining the target state.

How can an enterprise avoid over-engineering AI & Intelligence?

Preserve stable capability, change the layer causing the constraint and use operating evidence before scaling. The aim is AI tied to real work, measurable output quality, and better use of enterprise context, not a larger technology footprint.
