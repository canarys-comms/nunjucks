Questions teams usually ask

Frequently asked questions about Data & Analytics

Key questions when planning Data & Analytics.

What does Data & Analytics mean in an enterprise context?

For Canarys, Data & Analytics means using source systems, data movement, modelling, governance and consumption to create a dependable path from operational data to shared metrics, analytics and decisions. The emphasis is a working operating model rather than technology adoption by itself.

Where should a Data & Analytics initiative start?

Start where teams reconcile the same metrics differently or pipelines are brittle and hard to observe is already creating visible cost, delay or risk. A bounded starting point makes dependencies easier to understand and outcomes easier to measure.

What capabilities are usually involved in Data & Analytics?

The mix varies, but common delivery areas include data architecture, data integration, data modelling, and BI and analytics. The right combination depends on which layer is actually constraining the target state.

How can an enterprise avoid over-engineering Data & Analytics?

Preserve stable capability, change the layer causing the constraint and use operating evidence before scaling. The aim is more trusted metrics, faster analysis, and reusable data models, not a larger technology footprint.
